# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent while examining evidence, accept constructive
feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and protect private or
security-sensitive information. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that do
not align with these expectations. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate
and documented privately where appropriate.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. Security reports belong in the process described by `SECURITY.md`.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
